In accordance with Criterion 2, three prompt versions were designed and tested. The prompt evolves from an unstructured naive query to a production XML-delimited prompt with strict grounding guardrails.

Version 1 (v1) • Rubric Score: 3.0 / 5.0 (Rubric Fail)

Baseline Direct Prompt (Naive / Unconstrained) (Zero-Shot Direct Injection)

Prompt Template:
Answer this student query using the FAQ. Query: {q} FAQ: {faq}
Generated Output Sample:
The last date to pay semester fees is 15 November without penalty. After that a late fee of Rs. 500 per week applies. Pay through the student portal under Fees > Pay Online.

Engineering Analysis: Lacks formal greeting, next-step guidance, and official helpdesk sign-off. High risk of blunt answers or hallucinations when queries fall outside the FAQ boundary. Fails 2 out of 5 rubric criteria.

Verdict: Needs Improvement (Rubric Score: 3/5)
Version 2 (v2) • Rubric Score: 4.0 / 5.0 (Partial Pass)

Role & Grounding Constraints Prompt (Constrained Persona Prompt)

Prompt Template:
You are a helpful university helpdesk assistant. Write a short, polite reply to the student using ONLY the approved FAQ below. Do not add facts, dates or amounts that are not in the FAQ. Query: {q} Approved FAQ: {faq}
Generated Output Sample:
Hello! The last date to pay semester fees is 15 November without penalty. After that a late fee of Rs. 500 per week applies. Pay through the student portal under Fees > Pay Online.

Engineering Analysis: Introduces conversational greeting and strict factual grounding ('ONLY the approved FAQ'). However, it lacks structured task decomposition, fails to mandate actionable next steps, and misses formal department sign-off.

Verdict: Good (Rubric Score: 4/5)
Version 3 (v3) • Rubric Score: 5.0 / 5.0 (Excellent / Full Pass)

Production XML Structured Task-Decomposed Prompt (Final Standard) (XML Task-Decomposed Guardrail Prompt)

Prompt Template:
<role>University helpdesk assistant drafting replies for staff approval.</role> <rules> 1. Use ONLY facts inside <faq>. 2. Never invent dates, fees, links or promises. 3. Under 100 words. 4. Plain text. </rules> <format> Start with "Dear Student,"; give the answer; add one next step; end with "Regards,\nStudent Helpdesk Team". </format> <faq>{faq}</faq> <query>{q}</query> Write the reply now.
Generated Output Sample:
Dear Student,

Thank you for contacting the Student Helpdesk. The last date to pay semester fees is 15 November without penalty. After that, a late fee of Rs. 500 per week applies. Payments can be completed online via the student portal under Fees > Pay Online using UPI, Net Banking, or Debit/Credit cards.

Next Steps: If this does not resolve your query, reply to this message and we will help further.

Regards,
Student Helpdesk Team

Engineering Analysis: Employs XML tag separation (, , , , ) to isolate context and eliminate prompt injection. Enforces zero hallucination, strict conciseness (< 100 words), mandatory next step, and professional sign-off. Achieves 100% pass across all test cases.

Verdict: Excellent (Rubric Score: 5/5 PASS)
